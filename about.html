About Us

SmartClassroom was created to help students stay organized and manage school responsibilities more easily. Many students struggle with keeping track of homework, exams, and important school activities.


Our goal is to provide students with one simple platform where they can create notes, set reminders, and stay focused throughout the school year. SmartClassroom is designed for students who want to reduce stress and improve academic success.
